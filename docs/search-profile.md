# Search profile

The default ranking is tuned for one personal search. This is a product brief, not a general measure of job quality or a prediction of hiring success.

## Target

- US remote DevOps, SRE, platform, cloud operations and infrastructure work.
- Hands-on ownership of infrastructure, delivery workflows and production reliability.
- AWS, Terraform, Ansible, CI/CD, Linux, automation and observability.
- Release/build engineering and RPM packaging are relevant adjacent roles.
- Prefer smaller to midsize SaaS companies when source metadata establishes that fit.
- Deprioritize Kubernetes-heavy specialization; an incidental Kubernetes requirement is a small tradeoff, not an exclusion.
- Prefer direct employer/ATS postings and avoid repeatedly reviewing copies of the same role.

A GTM/business-systems title combined with Salesforce/ERP platform specialization is treated as off-target even when cloud keywords are present. An infrastructure title that merely mentions integrations with those tools retains its normal ranking.

## Daily workflow

1. Check collection health and a small shortlist of newly found roles.
2. See why each role fits, plus caveats such as contract work or unconfirmed US eligibility.
3. Read the full description, then save, dismiss with a reason, or mark applied.
4. Return to jobs found since the previous visit.
5. Follow useful employer boards so coverage survives unreliable search indexing.

## Unknowns and interpretation

There is no configured salary floor, preferred seniority, state eligibility or work authorization rule. The system must not invent those constraints. A US country match does not establish eligibility in every state; read the posting for residency, timezone and authorization requirements.

The dashboard and shortlist default to confirmed US country evidence. Clearing “US confirmed” includes generic remote roles that need further verification. Raw eligibility remains a geography/workplace classification; a low ranking can still indicate poor role fit.

A shortlist window refers to **first discovery**, not necessarily first publication. Known postings older than 30 days are omitted from the shortlist. Unknown publication dates are labeled, not fabricated. Identical company/title/location combinations are grouped conservatively within the shortlist and suppressed when a copy has been reviewed; distinct teams with identical titles and locations can be grouped as a limitation.

The defaults reflect the original project brief recovered from the owner's March 2026 planning conversation, the existing ranking rules, and subsequent clarification that AWS infrastructure also serves as portfolio evidence. Personal résumé files and conversation transcripts are not part of this repository.
